A possible day, not a plan

These sketches show the kinds of rhythms each setting could support. Your answers below will help organizers decide which rhythm fits the group best.

Example Auburn rhythm

Home base, lake, and group time

  • AM
    Slow breakfast

    Coffee, Vachan reading option, and a low-pressure morning at the house.

  • Midday
    Lake or foothills outing

    A few hours outside, then back before people get too tired.

  • PM
    Group dinner

    Cooking together, games, conversations, and unhurried hangout time.

  • Late
    Cozy night in

    Cards, board games, movie night, or open mic in the house.

Example Paicines rhythm

Trail time, quiet, and stars

  • AM
    Early trail option

    A sunrise or cooler-morning hike for people who want a bigger nature day.

  • Midday
    Pinnacles exploration

    High Peaks, caves, a photo walk, or a lighter outdoor route.

  • PM
    Rest and dinner

    Regroup at the house with food, free time, and slower conversation.

  • Late
    Stargazing

    Clear-sky time outside, reflection, or a calm evening together.
